Net GEX
—

Total gamma exposure across all strikes. Positive net GEX usually means dealers are net long gamma and may buy dips / sell rips, which can dampen volatility. Negative net GEX suggests the opposite — moves may amplify.

Call Wall
—

The strike with the heaviest call-side gamma exposure. Price often gravitates toward this level as dealers hedge; it is commonly watched as a potential ceiling or resistance zone.

Put Wall
—

The strike with the heaviest put-side gamma exposure. Dealer hedging around this level can slow selloffs, so it is often treated as a potential floor or support zone.

Spot Price
—

The underlying’s last price when this snapshot was taken. Compare spot to the call and put walls to see whether price is trading between, above, or below key gamma levels.
